# Art direction

## Concept: old-master materiality meets conservation science

1. **Chosen - Archival examination room.** Near-black ultramarine ground, gilded gold accents and italic serif labels for the "old master" half of the brief; thin hairline conservation-frame labels for the "science lab" half. Reference: a conservation studio's raking-light examination table, not a museum wall label.
2. Rejected - a literal parchment-colored background: tested, but it fought with every imaging mode's own palette (UV teal, infrared gray, X-ray contrast); a dark ground lets each modality's color read cleanly.
3. Rejected - a top navigation bar (the pattern most other blueprints in the volume use): explicitly rejected per the book's "navigation labels live in the conservation frame rather than a top bar."

## Palette (from the blueprint, used verbatim)
Ultramarine `#153B73`, Gilded Gold `#C6A15B`, Parchment `#DED1B7`, Varnish Brown `#4D3324`, Conservation Green `#587061`, Infrared Red `#922F39`.

## Typography
Serif italic for labels and captions (echoing EIDOLON's editorial register but gold rather than red-accented, and without EIDOLON's full magazine layout), monospace only for the brand mark and the mode uniform's technical register.

## Component grammar
No panels, no top bar: every label is positioned directly against the frame edges around the painting. The third blueprint in the volume (after LUCENT, after EIDOLON's partial version) to abandon the shared `.panel` component entirely.
